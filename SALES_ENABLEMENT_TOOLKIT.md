## CUSTOMER OBJECTION HANDLING

```
Objection Statement:
"We have an internal audit team that handles fraud risk. We don't 
need an external assessment."

Handling Approach:

1. VALIDATE:
"That's great. Having an internal team is important. My question 
is: how structured is your internal process? Is it documented in 
a way auditors would recognize?"

2. PROBE:
- "Do you follow a specific framework like GovS-013?"
- "Can you easily trend fraud risk over time?"
- "Do you have documented evidence for compliance purposes?"

3. POSITION:
"What START FRA provides is three things:

a) Government-aligned framework (GovS-013, ECCTA 2023)
   - Auditors immediately recognize it
   - Reduces back-and-forth on compliance

b) Standardized assessment
   - Your internal team can use our framework
   - Saves development time
   - Benchmarked against other organizations

c) Ongoing monitoring if you want it
   - We can do annual assessments
   - Or quarterly if you prefer ongoing visibility

Think of us as your 'second opinion' framework that comes with 
professional credibility attached."

4. CLOSE:
"Would it be valuable to do a one-off professional assessment 
(£795) as a benchmark? Then you'd know how your internal process 
compares to government standards."
```

## DISCOVERY QUESTIONS PLAYBOOK

### Opening Discovery

```
"Before I recommend a package, I want to understand your situation 
better. I'll ask a few questions, then I can tailor my recommendation. 
Sound good?"

1. Organization
   Q: "Tell me about your organization. What do you do?"
   Why: Understand sector, complexity, regulatory environment

2. Size & Growth
   Q: "How many employees do you have currently? Any growth plans?"
   Why: Determines package directly

3. Current State
   Q: "What's your current approach to fraud prevention?"
   Why: Understand starting point, pain points

4. Primary Pain
   Q: "What's the biggest fraud-related challenge you face?"
   Why: Uncover true need, not assumed need

5. Compliance
   Q: "Are you subject to compliance audits or regulatory oversight?"
   Why: Determines whether compliance-ready features are critical

6. Success Criteria
   Q: "If we implement fraud risk assessment, what would success look like?"
   Why: Define what winning means (helps with close later)

7. Timeline
   Q: "When would you want to implement this?"
   Why: Understand urgency, buying timeline

8. Decision Process
   Q: "Who else needs to be involved in this decision?"
   Why: Know if you need more stakeholders, approvals, etc.
```

### Deep Dive Discovery (if Enterprise prospect)

```
ADDITIONAL QUESTIONS FOR ENTERPRISE PROSPECTS:

       how would you use it?"
   Why: Understand dashboard value, use case depth

3. Scale
   Q: "How do you currently manage fraud awareness across 500+ people?"
   Why: Understand current challenges, staffing

4. Reporting
   Q: "How does your organization report compliance status to leadership?"
   Why: Board-ready reports feature, governance artifact

5. Support
   Q: "What level of vendor support is important to you?"
   Why: Priority support is Enterprise differentiator
```
