# Special Marketing Agent - Home Page Rebuild

## Purpose
Rebuild the home page to maximize marketing impact and conversion while strictly preserving the Government Digital Service (GDS) design standards and UI/UX established in the existing codebase.

## Agent Constraints & Requirements

### ✅ MUST PRESERVE
1. **Government Design System (GDS)**
   - UK Civil Service design patterns
   - Accessibility standards (WCAG AA minimum)
   - Font: Century Gothic / GDS approved typefaces
   - Color scheme: UK government palette (greens, whites, blacks)
   - Component library: Existing shadcn/ui components

2. **Existing UI Components**
   - Button styles (primary, outline, ghost variants)
   - Card components (CardContent, CardHeader, CardTitle)
   - Badge styling
   - Form inputs and selects
   - Navigation patterns

3. **Branding Elements**
   - START FRA logo and identity
   - Compliance badges (GovS-013, ECCTA 2023)
   - Shield icons and trust indicators
   - Mono-spaced fonts for technical data

4. **Responsive Design**
   - Mobile-first approach
   - Tablet optimization
   - Desktop experience
   - Existing breakpoint system

### ❌ DO NOT
- Change underlying component library
- Alter core color palette
- Remove accessibility features
- Break existing routes/links
- Modify animation libraries (Framer Motion)
- Insert advertisement networks
- Add analytics beyond Google Analytics

## Design System Adherence

### Color Palette (Government Approved)
```
Primary:      #0b7e1f (Government Green)
Primary Alt:  #005a30 (Dark Green)
Accent:       #f3802f (Amber/Gold - call-to-action)
Danger:       #d4351c (Red - alerts)
Neutral:      #f3f2f1 (Off-white - backgrounds)
Text:         #0b0c0c (Official Black)
Muted:        #626a6e (Gray - secondary text)
```

### Typography (GDS Standards)
```
Headlines:  Bold Segoe UI / Arial (sans-serif)
Body:       Regular Lato / Open Sans
Technical:  Mono (code data)
Font-size:  Minimum 16px (mobile), 18px (desktop)
Line-height: 1.5 (body), 1.2 (headlines)
```

### Spacing (8px Grid)
```
Margin/Padding: Multiples of 8px
Sections:       32px (mobile), 64px (desktop)
Cards:          16px internal
Buttons:        8px-24px
```

### Accessibility Requirements
```
✅ Minimum WCAG AA compliance
✅ Color contrast ratio 4.5:1 (text)
✅ Keyboard navigation throughout
✅ Screen reader friendly (semantic HTML)
✅ Focus indicators visible
✅ Mobile touch targets ≥44px
✅ Alt text on all images
✅ ARIA labels where needed
```

## Pricing Scripts - Common Objections

### Objection 1: "That's Too Expensive"
**Why They Say It:**
- Budget constraints
- Price comparison with competitors
- Not yet convinced of ROI
- Internal approval needed

**Response Strategy:**
```
Acknowledge → Reframe → Value-Prove → Help-Decide

"I understand – compliance tools can feel like an investment.
But think of it this way: One instance of failed fraud screening 
can cost £50,000+ in claims alone. START FRA pays for itself 
with a single prevented loss. Plus, at £795 one-time for Starter, 
we're the most affordable option on the market.

Would it help if I showed you the ROI calculator for your 
organization size? That usually surprises people."
```

**Package-Specific Angles:**
- **Starter (£795):** "Less than a month of one person's salary"
- **Professional (£1,799/year):** "£150/month for unlimited staff training"
- **Enterprise (£4,995/year):** "£5K covers 50-500 employees – less than £10 per employee annually"

---

### Objection 2: "We Don't Have Budget for This Right Now"
**Why They Say It:**
- Fiscal year/budget cycle constraint
- Need approval process
- Emergency funding required
- Competing priorities

**Response Strategy:**
```
Acknowledge → Flexibility → Timeline → Action

"I completely understand – the budget cycle is tight.
Here's what I typically recommend: Start with our Starter 
assessment (£795 one-time, no annual commitment). This gets 
your baseline risk score, proof of compliance, and you can 
build the case for ongoing monitoring with your CFO.

Then when next year's budget opens, you're ready to upgrade 
with your baseline already done. Does that timeline work better?"
```

**Flexibility Messaging:**
- "Starter is a one-time payment – no annual commitment"
- "Start in Q2 if that works with your budget cycle"
- "Payment plans available for Professional/Enterprise"

---

### Objection 3: "Why Should We Use START FRA vs. [Competitor Name]?"
**Why They Say It:**
- Comparison shopping
- Need vendor justification
- Risk aversion (sticking with known competitor)
- Looking for differentiation

**Response Strategy:**
```
Acknowledge → Differentiate → Prove → Invite

"Great question – let me show you what makes us different:

Most competitors are either:
1. Expensive consultancy firms (£15K+, 8-week turnaround)
2. Generic SaaS platforms (don't address fraud specifically)
3. Spreadsheet solutions (no real-time monitoring)

We're the only one that combines:
✓ Government-grade compliance (GovS-013, ECCTA 2023)
✓ Affordable pricing (£795 starting point)
✓ Production-ready dashboard (Enterprise)
✓ Assessment + ongoing monitoring + training

Why not run a comparison? I can do a 15-minute demo 
showing your specific data against [competitor]."
```

**Competitive Positioning:**
- **vs. Consultancies:** "10x cheaper, 100x faster, online not offline"
- **vs. Generic SaaS:** "Fraud-specific, not general risk"
- **vs. Spreadsheets:** "Real-time monitoring, audit trails, scale"

---

- Internal stakeholder approval needed
- Need business case document
- Formal approval process required
- ROI proof needed

```
"Perfect – let me help you build that case internally.
Most approvals need three things:

1. ROI Calculation: One prevented fraud incident

2. Compliance Requirement: GovS-013 and ECCTA 2023

3. Implementation Timeline: We get you live in 1 week,

I can send you:
- Executive 1-pager (2 mins to explain to CFO)
- Full ROI calculator (customize for your org size)
- Compliance mapping (how we meet your standards)
- Case study (similar organization, revenue impact)

What format would be most useful for your approver?"
```

**Documentation Provided:**

- ROI calculator template

### Objection 5: "Do I Really Need [This Feature] Right Now?"
**Why They Say It:**
- Questioning specific package features
- Concern about over-purchasing
- Wanting to start minimal
- Not seeing immediate need

**Response Strategy:**
```
Acknowledge → Clarify → Recommend → Offer

"Good thinking – let's make sure you're not paying for 
what you don't need right now.

Tell me:
- How many employees do you have?
- Are you currently monitoring fraud risk?
- Do you need ongoing assessments or just a baseline?

Based on your answers, here's what I'd recommend:

[Suggest appropriate package with 2-3 key reasons]

But if you want to start conservative and upgrade later, 
that's fine too – we support package upgrades anytime."
```

**Package Recommendation Matrix:**
- **Starter:** <50 employees OR just need baseline
- **Professional:** 50-250 employees OR need staff training
- **Enterprise:** 250+ employees OR need real-time monitoring/API
